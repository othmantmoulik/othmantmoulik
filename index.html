Common Questions

Everything You Need to Know

How much does a basement secondary suite cost in Ottawa?
Most full basement-to-secondary-suite conversions in Ottawa range from $80,000 to $150,000 depending on size, scope, and whether underpinning is required. During your free assessment, we provide a detailed fixed-price quote — so you know exactly what you're investing before you commit.
Is it legal to have a secondary suite in Ottawa?
Yes. The City of Ottawa permits secondary dwelling units in most residential zones. In fact, recent bylaw changes have made it easier than ever. Our engineering team ensures your suite meets all Ontario Building Code requirements and passes every city inspection.
Why do I need a licensed engineer? Can't any contractor do this?
Any structural modification — removing load-bearing walls, underpinning, adding separate HVAC systems — requires P.Eng-stamped drawings by Ontario law. Most contractors outsource this, adding weeks and thousands in fees. Our engineers are in-house, which saves you time and money while ensuring everything is done to the highest standard.
How long does the entire process take?
From your initial assessment to handing you the keys: typically 12-16 weeks total. That breaks down to 2-4 weeks for permits and engineering, and 8-12 weeks for construction. We provide a detailed project timeline during your assessment.
What return on investment can I expect?
A legal secondary suite in Ottawa typically rents for $1,800–$2,500/month. Most of our clients see a full return on their investment within 3-5 years — while their property value increases by $100K-$200K immediately. It's one of the highest-ROI home improvements you can make.
Do you offer financing?
We've partnered with lenders who specialize in home improvement financing. Many clients use a HELOC or refinance, which often means your monthly rental income exceeds your loan payment from day one — making the suite cash-flow positive immediately.
